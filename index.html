Biography

Mapping the World, One Pixel at a Time

I am a professional GIS & Remote Sensing Specialist with over 7 years of experience transforming spatial data into operational intelligence. My core competence bridges rigorous desktop analytics, automated processing loops, and modern web infrastructure to resolve demanding geometric, environmental, and infrastructure puzzles.

Throughout my professional track, I have successfully piloted nationwide scale data auditing routines, spearheaded precision UAV aerial surveys, managed foundational land administration systems, and engineered targeted spatial statistics configurations. Currently, I deploy these methods at the Bangladesh Forest Department under the World Bank-funded SUFAL Project — leading comprehensive quality control routines for the regular maintenance of the Sustainable Forest and Livelihood Control Program — to dramatically improve data accuracy.

"What gets me excited is using geospatial tools to drive real impact — whether spotting hidden patterns, streamlining operations, or helping make smarter policy decisions. Technical precision + strategic thinking + a bit of creativity = results that matter."

Happy to put maps to work on something meaningful — minus the projection drama.
